9. Cancellation and consumer rights

Uganda’s Electronic Transactions Act provides seven-day cancellation rights and refunds within thirty days of qualifying cancellation. It also provides remedies for missing required disclosures and late or unavailable supply. Statutory rights take priority over these terms, return conditions and website status controls.

You may notify cancellation by email or WhatsApp using your order reference. A website approval step is an administrative process, not a condition that removes a legal right. See the Return Policy and Refund page for instructions.
