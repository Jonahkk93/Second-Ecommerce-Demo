Delivery in Uganda

MPWR currently sells and delivers only within Uganda. International orders and delivery are not offered.

Enter your district and delivery address at checkout. Your delivery fee and estimated delivery window appear before you continue to payment.

Getting your address ready

Include your street, building or a useful landmark, city or town, district, and a phone number. Use delivery notes for directions that help locate your address.

You can save addresses in your account and choose a default for future orders.
