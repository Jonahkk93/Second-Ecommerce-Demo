How To Track My Order

About MPWR / How To Track My Order

MPWR · Shopping & information

Your order’s progress is in your account.

Find your order

  1. Sign in to the account you used when placing the order.
  2. Open My Account and select Order Status.
  3. Find your order using its items, date, or reference.
  4. Read the status and the delivery updates shown on the order.

What the status means

Pending
The order is awaiting the next processing step. Check its payment information as well.
Processing
The order is being prepared.
Shipped
The order has moved to delivery.
Delivered
The order is marked as delivered.
Cancelled or Returned
Check the order details and any refund panel for further information.

Delivery updates

The order page shows tracking details when they have been added to your order. Check it again for the latest recorded update.
