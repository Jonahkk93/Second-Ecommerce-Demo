About MPWR / Payment Method

MPWR · Shopping & information

Choose how you would like to pay at checkout.

Available options

  • Credit or debit card — enter your card details on the payment provider’s checkout.
  • MTN MoMo — use your MTN Mobile Money number.
  • Airtel Money — use your Airtel Money number.

Your order total is shown in Ugandan shillings (USh). Available payment options are confirmed during secure payment.

Complete secure payment

Review your contact details, delivery address, selected payment method, and order total. Choose Continue to secure payment and follow the payment provider’s authorization steps.

Return to MPWR when prompted, then check your payment result and Order Status. If the result is unclear, check the order before trying another payment.

Saved payment preferences

My Payment Options lets you save or remove payment preferences and set a default. Saving a preference does not make a payment; you still authorize payment when ordering.
