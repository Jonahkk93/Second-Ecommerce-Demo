MPWR · Shopping & information

From finding your favourites to completing payment.

1. Find your item

Browse wigs, press-on nails, lashes, and other available products. Open an item to see its description, images, available options, price, and customer reviews.

2. Make it yours

Select the available options you want, choose a quantity, and add the item to your cart. Check the selected options and price in your cart before continuing.

3. Add your delivery details

Sign in and open checkout from your cart. Enter your contact information, address, city or town, and district. Check the delivery fee and estimate before proceeding.

4. Review and pay

Choose card, MTN MoMo, or Airtel Money. Review your items and the full total, then choose Continue to secure payment. Follow the payment provider’s prompts to authorize the transaction.

5. Follow your order

Return to MPWR after payment. Use Order Status to check the payment and order progress. Your purchases also appear in My Purchases.
