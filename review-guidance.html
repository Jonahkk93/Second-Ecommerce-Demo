MPWR · Shopping & information

Share your experience and help other shoppers.

Review a purchased item

  1. Sign in to the account used for your purchase.
  2. Open My Purchases and select the Review tab.
  3. Choose Review item for an eligible purchase.
  4. Choose a star rating, add a comment, and post your review.

Product reviews are available for purchased items. Cancelled and returned orders are excluded from review eligibility.

Add useful details

Describe the item you received and your experience using it. Details about the selected size, length, colour, or finish can help other shoppers choose.

You can add up to five JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF photos, each up to 5 MB. Use clear images that you have permission to share.

Keep personal details private

Your product review may be publicly visible. Leave out contact details, payment information, passwords, and other people’s private information.

Website suggestions

Use Feedback & Rating for suggestions about finding products, product information, checkout, and website errors. Product reviews are for your experience with the item itself.
